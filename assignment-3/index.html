Welcome to McGraw Tutoring!

🐯 Arriving at McGraw Tutoring

The McGraw Center is located on the second floor of the Frist Campus Center.

  • Bring your prox ID to check in
  • Check in with a member of the admin team
  • They will direct you to the appropriate study hall room

📊 Dashboard

View live tutor and student counts for each class study hall session.

Each card shows how busy a class is and whether it is open, busy, or full.

This can help you get a sense of how long you might have to wait for a tutor and decide which session to attend.

  • 🟢 Open = low student-to-tutor ratio
  • 🟠 Busy = moderate student-to-tutor ratio
  • 🔴 Full = high student-to-tutor ratio
  • ⚪ Closed = no tutors available

💬 Chat

Use the chat page to ask questions, share what you're working on, and coordinate with others in your class.

  • Sign in with your Google account to send messages
  • Select your class from the dropdown
  • Send messages to others in that class
  • See live updates instantly
